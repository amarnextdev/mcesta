Order processing

  • Please double-check your address at checkout. If a package is returned because the address was wrong, we can re-ship it, but [the second shipment is charged to you].

01

Damaged, defective or wrong items

Arrived damaged or wrong?Report it within 48 hours of delivery with an unboxing video or clear photos.Report a problem

We cover every item that arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered. To help us resolve it quickly:

  • Report it within 48 hours of delivery from your order page or by email.
  • Include an unboxing video, or clear photos of the outer box, the shipping label and the item.
  • Keep the item and packaging until your claim is settled.

Once approved, you choose a replacement or a full refund, including shipping. [Most of the time you won't need to send the item back.]

02

Change-of-mind returns

Changed your mind? You can return eligible items within [3] days of delivery. The window starts on the day tracking shows your order as delivered.

  • The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with all parts and tags.
  • Contact us first to get a return authorisation and the return address. Packages sent without one can't be accepted.
  • Return shipping is paid by [you / us].
03

Items that can't be returned

These items are final sale unless they arrive damaged or wrong:

  • Personalised or made-to-order items.
  • Opened beauty tools and personal care items, for hygiene reasons.
  • Gift cards.
  • Items marked "Final sale" on the product page.
04

How to start a return

  1. 1Contact usUse "Report a problem" on your order, or email [support@yourdomain.com].
  2. 2Get approvalWe reply within [1 business day] with next steps.
  3. 3Send it backFor returns, pack the item and use the label or address we give you.
  4. 4Get your refundWe refund you once the return is received and checked.
05

Refunds

  • Refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take [5–10] business days to show it.
  • For damaged or wrong items, we refund the item and the shipping you paid.
  • For change-of-mind returns, the original shipping cost is [not refundable].
  • We'll email you when your refund is issued.
06

Cancelling an order

You can cancel within [2 hours] of placing your order, or any time before it ships, for a full refund. Once an order has shipped, it follows the return rules above.

07

Talk to us first

Something wrong? Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank. We can usually fix it in a day, and a dispute can take weeks.

This policy doesn't affect your rights under the consumer laws where you live.
